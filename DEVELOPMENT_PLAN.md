# AVICARE PRO Development Plan

## Phase 1 — Foundation
- Audit and stabilize the current theme structure.
- Document reusable sections, snippets, and blocks.
- Align all future work with OS 2.0 architecture and project rules.

## Phase 2 — Design system
- Define typography, spacing, button styles, cards, and component patterns.
- Establish the premium veterinary visual language with the AVICARE PRO brand palette.
- Prepare reusable tokens and shared UI patterns.

## Phase 3 — Component architecture
- Define reusable AVICARE PRO components for layout, buttons, cards, ecommerce blocks, and brand-specific modules.
- Align components with the design system and the existing Atelier architecture.
- Prepare a scalable structure for future section and snippet implementation.

## Phase 4 — Header and footer
- Refine header navigation, mobile menu, localization, and cart experience.
- Build a premium footer structure with strong brand trust signals.
- Ensure full Arabic RTL support.

## Phase 4 — Homepage
- Create a branded homepage with strong hero storytelling and conversion paths.
- Use reusable sections and product highlights.
- Prioritize clarity, trust, and high-intent CTA placement.

## Phase 5 — Product page
- Improve product presentation, pricing, trust content, and purchase confidence.
- Ensure variant selection, media behavior, and add-to-cart flows are polished.
- Support premium product storytelling and educational content.

## Phase 6 — Collection pages
- Refine collection browsing, filtering, and product card presentation.
- Optimize for discovery and conversion.
- Ensure responsive and RTL-friendly layout behavior.

## Phase 7 — Science page
- Develop a science-led content experience that explains formulation and credibility.
- Use strong editorial presentation and trust-building visuals.

## Phase 8 — Programs page
- Create a dedicated experience for product programs, bundles, or customer journeys.
- Support marketing and conversion goals through structured content.

## Phase 9 — Conversion optimization
- Improve CTA hierarchy, trust modules, urgency, and checkout intent.
- Refine product page and cart interactions for higher conversion.

## Phase 10 — Production release
- Final QA, localization review, performance checks, and launch preparation.
- Confirm theme is stable across mobile, desktop, and Arabic RTL.
