# AVICARE PRO Brand Guide

## Brand overview
- Brand: AVICARE PRO
- Industry: Premium veterinary pigeon supplements
- Market focus: GCC and global premium wellness market
- Primary languages: English and Arabic
- Direction: Luxury veterinary science with a modern, trustworthy, high-performance identity

## Brand personality
AVICARE PRO should feel:
- Scientific
- Premium
- Professional
- Trustworthy
- Performance focused

## Brand tone
Use a tone that is calm, confident, precise, and evidence-led. Messaging should feel expert and premium without becoming overly clinical or promotional.

## Visual direction
The design direction should reflect:
- Luxury veterinary science
- Clean, modern layouts
- High trust and credibility
- Premium commerce experiences
- Strong visual hierarchy for conversion

## Color system
### Primary
- #0F4D3A

### Gold accent
- #D4AF37

### Text
- #1A1A1A

## Design principles
- Keep the experience elegant and refined.
- Emphasize clarity, trust, and product credibility.
- Use premium spacing, typography, and imagery.
- Make the brand feel modern and science-led rather than purely luxury-driven.

## Content direction
- Highlight efficacy, quality, and veterinary credibility.
- Present products in a polished, premium, and reassuring way.
- Support Arabic and English content gracefully with strong RTL layout handling.
